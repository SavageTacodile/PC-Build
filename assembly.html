Installing the CPU

It's a good practice to place the motherboard on top of the motherboard's box to work on it. This protects the underside from scratches. Open the CPU socket and find the little arrow mark on the border of it. Line the same arrow mark on your CPU with the arrow mark on the socket. GENTLY drop the CPU in. Don't force it. You shouldn't ever have to push on a CPU. Once it's laying in completely flat, close the retention arm to secure it in the socket.
