Key Components of a PC Build

Every PC is made from a core set of components that are mostly standardized to fit together.

  • Motherboard
  • CPU
  • RAM
  • Power Supply
  • Solid State Drive
  • PC Case/Chassis
  • CPU Cooler
  • Case Fans
  • Graphics Card
  • Windows Operating System
  • Included PSU Cables
  • Keyboard and Mouse

Why PC Component Choice Matters

The parts that you choose to build with will determine the speed, graphical power, and processing power of your experience. If you want a PC for gaming, you should focus most of your budget on your GPU and CPU. If you need a PC for just standard everyday tasks, you don't have to put much thought into the power of your components unless you're working with much older generations of components. If you're buying from a well-known distributor such as Micro Center, you won't really have to worry about generations that are too old.
